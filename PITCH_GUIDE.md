# Influence — 90-second pitch flow

## The opening

“Creator marketing is full of vanity metrics. Brands can see who has followers, but not who will create trust, conversion, and measurable business impact. Influence finds that signal.”

## The live walkthrough

1. **Identity:** Create an account with a name, email, and password. Point out that the greeting, avatar, and full profile page use that account name. The bundled demo account appears as Manami Bera.
2. **Overview:** Show the executive metrics, campaign impact chart, and green AI insight card. Explain that Influence connects creator activity to earned media value and ROAS.
3. **New AI match:** Click **New AI match**, keep the prepared brief, and click **Find best-fit creators**. The scan reads intent, verifies audience quality, and predicts fit through an authenticated server route.
4. **Explain and act:** Open Riya Ray from Discover. Show the authenticity, relevance, and conversion scores, then assign or remove her from a campaign directly in the profile.
5. **Campaign operations:** Open a campaign workspace. Assign another creator, remove one, advance the milestone, and show the creator count update immediately.
6. **Live app chat:** Open Riya’s in-app chat in two browsers signed into different accounts. Send a message and show it appear in the other browser without refreshing.
7. **Finish with proof:** Open Analytics. Point to creator-attributed revenue, audience quality, and the learning engine that recalculates from campaign state.

## Closing line

“Influence does not help brands buy attention. It helps them find credible voices, move culture, and prove the result.”

## Likely judge questions

**How is the match score calculated?**  
It combines audience authenticity, campaign relevance, historical conversion signal, and budget efficiency. Each component is exposed so a brand can challenge the recommendation.

**How do you detect fake influence?**  
Influence compares follower growth, engagement quality, posting consistency, audience geography, and peer benchmarks. Suspicious patterns are surfaced as risks rather than hidden inside one score.

**What is the business model?**  
Tiered SaaS for brands and agencies, plus a small transaction fee when campaign payments use the platform workflow.

**What is defensible?**  
The outcome data. Every completed campaign improves the link between creator behaviour and real conversion, creating a proprietary feedback loop that follower databases cannot copy.
